“The best heritage experience is one that makes you curious enough to keep exploring.”
QUIZ TIPS

How to Get the Most From a Round

Use the quiz as part of your learning journey rather than treating it only as a score.

Read Carefully

Look for the exact place, period, person or idea being asked about before choosing an answer.

Think First

Pause for a moment and connect the question with what you remember from the learning content.

Learn From Mistakes

A wrong answer is useful when it helps you notice what you should explore next.
