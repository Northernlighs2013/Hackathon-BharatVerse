THE CHALLENGE

India's Heritage Is Hard to Discover.

Heritage information is often fragmented across different sources, making it difficult for young people to explore India's history in a connected and engaging way.

🧩

Fragmented Discovery

Heritage information is spread across too many sources, making it difficult to build a clear understanding of India's past.

😴

Low Engagement

Traditional formats can make history feel distant instead of turning it into an experience.

📚

Missed Learning

Young audiences need a simple way to connect monuments, dynasties, festivals and living culture.

🌍

Access Gaps

New learners and visitors need an intuitive place to discover where their journey through Indian heritage should begin.
